Problem statement 6 of 6

Construction Progress and Resource Control

Connecting the phased layouts to sequencing, resources and honest progress measurement.

Constructed case studyConstructed academic case study — prepared to complete the portfolio; not represented as an original submitted problem statement.

On this pageScenarioFrameworkMeasurementCoordinationRisk registerMinutesConclusionEvidence
01

Scenario

Develop a project-control framework for the seven-block mall in PS1, linking the phased site-layout concept to work sequencing, labour, materials, equipment and progress reporting.

Turn scope into a work breakdown structure (WBS)
Baseline programme once design and approvals exist
Track planned vs actual progress
Find constraints before they delay work fronts
Coordinate labour, materials, plant, subcontractors
Report risks, changes and corrective actions
02

Planning framework

  1. Confirm drawings, scope, milestones
  2. Block- and discipline-wise work packages
  3. Predecessors and access constraints
  4. Logic-linked baseline programme
  5. Link procurement and labour to activities
  6. Long-lead materials and critical approvals
  7. Update progress at agreed frequency
  8. Investigate variance; agree recovery
The PS1 drawings show phased arrangements but give no durations or programme. No dates, completion percentages or critical-path results are shown.
03

Progress measurement

QuantityQuantity-based progress = Installed quantity ÷ Approved total quantity
MilestonesCompletion of defined, verifiable milestones
Schedule varianceDifference between planned and actual dates or progress
Look-aheadConstraints for the next two to six weeks
Site recordsDaily reports, inspection requests, delivery records, measurement sheets
Measurement rule
Use consistent measurement rules. Physical progress, payment progress and expenditure progress are different metrics and must not be mixed.

07

Conclusion

Reliable project control links scope, programme, procurement, workforce, equipment, quality and safety. This is a monitoring framework, not a report of actual progress or schedule performance.
